How the bot works

• Average Caleb reuses legal moves from matching positions in the PGNs and otherwise samples from engine candidates using Caleb’s move patterns.

• Peak Caleb searches deeper and chooses the top engine candidate, with a small style adjustment based on Caleb’s games.

Style imitation is not a neural network trained from scratch. Its strength is not officially calibrated to a Chess.com rating; the “average” setting is intended to approximate Caleb's typical historical play, not guarantee a particular Elo.
